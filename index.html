Pick up & play

One thumb. Total control.

No buttons, no clutter — just drag. The controls were built to feel light and instant, so the bunny goes exactly where your finger leads.

1

Drag to move — in every direction

Slide your finger to steer left, right, up and down. It's full 2D movement, so you can climb the meadow or drop back to thread a gap.

2

The bunny rides above your thumb

Your character floats just above your fingertip, so your hand never hides the action or the hazard you're dodging.

3

Flick for a burst of speed

A quick swipe gives an extra nudge of momentum — perfect for last-second dodges around rocks and trees.

4

Read the telegraphs

Chevrons warn you near the horizon and a red flash + screen-shake tells you when you've taken a hit. Brief invulnerability gives you room to recover.

✨ Lives, power-ups & more

3 Lives & Hearts

Start each run with three lives. Take a hit and you get a moment of invulnerability to recover. Grab a heart to heal.

Power-Ups

Pop a shield bubble, slip into slow-motion, or double your points with a ×Score boost.

Revive with Diamonds

Out of lives? Spend a diamond to spring back into action with a short protective shield and keep your streak alive.
